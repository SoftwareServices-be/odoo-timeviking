Timesheet report on customer invoices

Part of TimeViking Timesheet Sync. Installed automatically when Sales Timesheet is installed next to it. When you confirm a customer invoice that bills timesheets, a PDF timesheet report with those hours (hours logged in TimeViking included) is attached to the invoice and sent with the invoice email. Turn it off in Settings › TimeViking › Connection, or attach it by hand with the Attach Timesheets button on the invoice.
