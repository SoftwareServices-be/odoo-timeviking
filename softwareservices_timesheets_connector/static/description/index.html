TimeViking Timesheet Sync

Fast weekly timesheets for your whole team, written straight into Odoo Timesheets

This free module connects your Odoo to TimeViking, an online timesheet service. Your team logs hours in a visual week calendar on the web or on their phone; the hours arrive in Odoo as timesheet lines on your projects and tasks.

Free connector Odoo 18, 19 and 20 Community and Enterprise

Requires a TimeViking account. This connector is free. It needs an account on TimeViking (https://timeviking.com), a separate web and mobile app for timesheets with its own subscription; see the website for the plans. The module only sends data to it after an administrator pairs Odoo with that account.

What your team gets

Log hours in seconds

Type 2h30 ABC login bug or 9-12:30 #website meeting and the project, task and duration fill themselves in. Or start a timer, or copy last week in one click.

A visual week calendar

Drag tasks into the week, stretch a block to change its duration, and see at a glance how each day compares with the planned hours. A grid view is there for those who prefer rows.

Excel and PDF exports

Monthly timesheets for customers or payroll in Excel or PDF. Design your own PDF layout with the drag-and-drop template editor: logo, colours, tables, signature.

Schedules and time off

Work schedules and public holidays set the daily target. With Odoo Time Off installed, employees see their balance and request time off; managers approve it in Odoo as usual.

On the phone too

An installable mobile web app for logging hours on the road or straight after a meeting.

Simple to roll out

Invite your colleagues, link them to their Odoo employee and they can start. Weeks can be submitted and approved before they count.

What the module does in Odoo

  • No API key to manage: paste a pairing code from the service and you are connected.
  • You decide the scope: choose the companies, and allow or block each employee.
  • Only what is needed: read projects, tasks and employees; write timesheet lines for allowed employees. There is no generic access to your database.
  • Signed requests (HMAC-SHA256 with timestamp and nonce) in both directions.
  • Time Off (optional): schedules, public holidays, allocations, balances and time-off requests. Approving and refusing stay in Odoo.
  • Unpair at any time from the same screen.

Linked employees

See who is linked, and switch off an employee with one click.

Get started in four steps

  1. Create an account on TimeViking (https://timeviking.com).
  2. Install this module in your Odoo (it needs Project and Timesheets; Time Off is optional).
  3. In the service, open Settings › Odoo and create a pairing code.
  4. In Odoo, open Settings › TimeViking, paste the code, pick your companies and allow your employees.

Works on your own server and on Odoo.sh. Odoo Online does not allow custom modules; there the service can connect with an API key instead.
